How to read this — and what to watch out for
  • Project mix drives the raw numbers. Monthly trackers get few changes, ad hocs and templates many. A team or market with lots of trackers looks better on "Change h / project" and "% of hours" without working differently. Compare within one Project Type, or use vs expected: actual change hours ÷ what comparable projects (same type & complexity) average. 1.00× = as expected; 1.50× = 50% more change work than comparable projects.
  • A few heavy projects can carry a whole group. Open the group and check its top projects before drawing a conclusion. Groups with fewer than 10 projects are greyed out — too small to read.
  • Rounds are only as good as Zoho logging. "Rounds" is the DIARY Client Changes count as recorded. A 0 can mean no changes or not logged, and logging habits can differ between PM teams — see the "0 rounds" column.
  • Hours are only as good as iTime booking. Only hours booked on "Client changes work" count. Changes booked as "Job set-up", "Validation" or "Other tasks on project" are invisible here, so real change effort is higher than shown.
  • Coverage is partial. Closed projects only (open jobs and programs have no Zoho data here — see "Not covered"), only our people's hours, only hours inside the iTime file. Client country = the ordering market, not the end client.
  • PM team is the channel, not the source. Changes come from the client and RE; the PM team passes them on. Use this to decide where to tighten the process (questionnaire freeze, fewer consolidated rounds, a change budget in the quote) — not to rank PMs.

Method & definitions
  • Scope — iTime rows whose Direct Manager (on that row) sits in the selected org root's tree. Moving people count only for the period they were in the org. Titles with Manager / Team Leader / Director are excluded.
  • iTime file — UTF-8 and Windows-1252 rows are both read (the 2026 file is two exports joined: Jan–May and Jun–Nov). Rows with 0 hours are ignored. Name fixes live in CONFIG.NAME_ALIASES.
  • Role — QA if the title contains "Quality Assurance", otherwise SW (SW Account Leads count as SW).
  • Net working hours — all logged hours minus leave & holidays (vacation, company holiday, illness, authorised/paid/unpaid leave, office closure). This is a share of logged time, not the BI chargeability formula.
  • Admin & management — Admin project, task "Administration and management". Training — Admin project, training / trainer tasks. Other non-project — any other Admin task and the GOH BG On Bench codes.
  • Closed projects — job number (hyphens removed) found in the Efficiency sheet; complexity, type, client country and timings come from there.
  • Not in Efficiency — grouped by naming rules (editable in CONFIG at the top of the script): Internal / SD dev (P-codes, 24-072885-*, NGIE, Kalever, Metaforms, Consultancy); Pooled admin jobs (names with "admin" or "Flex jobs"); Programs (Coca-Cola/Coke, FRS, BAT, JTI, PMI, Imperial, MTN, Essilor, Pernod Ricard, BP); everything else is Open / other projects.
  • Complexity comparison — per JobBook, only hours logged by in-scope people inside the iTime file. Projects that started programming before the data year (the year holding most of the file's hours) miss their earlier hours, so they are off by default; the comparisons in Key insights and Client changes use the same rule.
  • Client changes — closed projects only. Change hours = iTime task "Client changes work" by in-scope people; rounds = Zoho DIARY Client Changes. Expected change hours per project = mean change hours of closed projects with the same type & complexity (≥ 5 projects; otherwise same type, otherwise all). "Total h vs comparable" = project hours ÷ median hours of the same peer group, averaged over the group's projects.
  • Phases — set-up = from SW assignment (or, when the assignment date is after the 1st link, the first hour logged on the job) to the 1st link to RE; changes = 1st link to launch; field = launch to the end of the close-field month. A person's pipeline = closed projects where they are lead SW/QA in Efficiency or logged ≥ 4 h. Open projects and programs have no dates, so a week can be "No tracked project" while the person still worked on them.
